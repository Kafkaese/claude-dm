# Bandit (CR 1/2)
Source: GameMastery Guide, NPC Gallery, Brigands (Paizo). https://legacy.aonprd.com/gameMasteryGuide/npcs/brigands.html (verified 2026-09-25)

**Bandit CR 1/2**
**XP** 200
Human warrior 2
CN Medium humanoid
**Init** +2; **Senses** Perception –1

**DEFENSE**
**AC** 17, touch 13, flat-footed 14 (+3 armor, +2 Dex, +1 dodge, +1 shield)
**hp** 11 (2d10)
**Fort** +3, **Ref** +2, **Will** –1

**OFFENSE**
**Speed** 30 ft.
**Melee** rapier +3 (1d6+1/18–20) or sap +3 (1d6+1 nonlethal)
**Ranged** composite longbow +4 (1d8+1/×3)

**STATISTICS**
**Str** 13, **Dex** 14, **Con** 11, **Int** 10, **Wis** 8, **Cha** 9
**Base Atk** +2; **CMB** +3; **CMD** 16
**Feats** Dodge, Point Blank Shot
**Skills** Climb +4, Handle Animal +3, Intimidate +3, Ride +5, Stealth +2
**Languages** Common
**Gear** studded leather, buckler, composite longbow (+1 Str) with 20 arrows, rapier, sap, light horse (combat trained)

## Quick notes
- Point Blank Shot: +1 attack and damage with the bow within 30 ft.
- Reskinned freely as sailors, salvagers or hired muscle (drop the horse).
- Used in: sandpoint-delve (Pell Cotter; Idra Salk's crewmen).
