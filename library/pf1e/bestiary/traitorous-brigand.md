# Traitorous Brigand (CR 2)
Source: NPC Codex p. 81 (Paizo). https://www.aonprd.com/NPCDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Traitorous%20Brigand (verified 2026-09-25)

**Traitorous Brigand CR 2**
**XP** 600
Half-orc fighter 3
NE Medium humanoid (human, orc)
**Init** +5; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception –1

**DEFENSE**
**AC** 17, touch 11, flat-footed 16 (+6 armor, +1 Dex)
**hp** 30 (3d10+9)
**Fort** +5, **Ref** +2, **Will** +2; +1 vs. fear
**Defensive Abilities** bravery +1, orc ferocity

**OFFENSE**
**Speed** 30 ft.
**Melee** mwk greataxe +7 (1d12+4/×3) or sap +6 (1d6+3 nonlethal)
**Ranged** javelin +4 (1d6+3)

**TACTICS**
The fighter opens with an Intimidate check to demoralize the nearest enemy, then wades in with his axe using Cleave when possible.

**STATISTICS**
**Str** 17, **Dex** 12, **Con** 14, **Int** 10, **Wis** 8, **Cha** 13
**Base Atk** +3; **CMB** +6; **CMD** 17
**Feats** Cleave, Improved Initiative, Iron Will, Power Attack
**Skills** Climb +5, Intimidate +9, Survival +3, Swim +5
**Languages** Common, Orc
**SQ** armor training 1, orc blood, weapon familiarity
**Combat Gear** potion of bull's strength, potion of cure moderate wounds, potion of enlarge person (CL 3rd)
**Other Gear** masterwork breastplate, javelins (4), masterwork greataxe, sap, sack, gold necklace (worth 100 gp), 124 gp

## Quick notes
- Power Attack: −1 attack, +3 damage with the two-handed greataxe (+7 → +6, 1d12+7).
- Orc ferocity: once per day, when brought below 0 hp but not killed, he can fight on for 1 more round as if disabled.
- Enlarge person (CL 3, 3 min): Large, reach 10 ft, +2 size Str, −2 size Dex, −1 attack and AC; greataxe becomes 3d6. Net while enlarged: greataxe +7 (3d6+6), AC 15. Token size 2, reach 10.
- AC line: the source lists +6 armor only but totals 17 with flat-footed 16, so the +1 Dex is included above.
- Used in: sandpoint-delve (Gorrun Brask; there, the enlarge potion is already used).
