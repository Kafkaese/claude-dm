# The Listening Lens (hazard, CR 1)
Publisher: Homebrew (sandpoint-delve). Built against `library/pf1e/rules/monster-creation.md` (CR 1 targets: primary DC 12, low damage 5). Magical hazard, not a creature.

**The Listening Lens CR 1**
**XP** 400
**Type** magic hazard; a 10-ft. disc of black glass in a bronze cradle, lit from within by pinpoints that are not reflections.

**Pulse (Su)** On initiative count 0 each round, the Lens pulses. Every creature within 10 ft. of the Lens (measured from its edge) that doesn't have total cover from it must make a DC 12 Will save or be **staggered for 1 round** ("for a heartbeat you hear the space between the stars"). A creature that fails by 5 or more is also **dazzled for 1 minute**. Mind-affecting. Creatures immune to mind-affecting effects are unaffected.

**Turning the Lens** A creature adjacent to the Lens can spend a standard action and succeed at a DC 15 Strength check, DC 15 Disable Device check, or DC 17 Knowledge (arcana) check to tilt the cradle. The next pulse is then a **30-ft. line** in a chosen direction instead of the ring: creatures in the line save as above and take 1d6 cold damage on a failed save.

**Covering the Lens** A full-round action while adjacent, using a cloak, sack, tarp or similar, stops the pulses until the covering is removed (a move action). This also ends the hazard for XP purposes.

**Opened** If the Starwheel is removed from the cradle, the Lens "opens": radius 15 ft., save DC 14.

**Night** When stars are in the sky above the shaft, save DC +2 (not cumulative with Opened beyond DC 14).

## Design check
- Save DC 12 = CR 1 primary DC. Damage 1d6 (avg 3.5) only on a turned pulse, below the CR 1 low-damage target of 5.
- Staggered is a strong condition against a solo PC, so the area is small, the rhythm is predictable (it always fires at initiative 0), and it is fully avoidable by position.
