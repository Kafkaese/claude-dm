# Movement (house rule)
**Table ruling** where PF1e is silent. `scripts/combat.py` implements it.

- **Diagonal count over the whole turn:** the 5-10-5 count (first diagonal 5 ft, second 10 ft, …) runs through all of a creature's movement in one turn: both moves of a double move, and separate stretches around a standard action. It starts over on the creature's next turn. The CRB (pg. 192) gives the alternation but doesn't say whether it carries over between moves; this is the common table practice.
- Forced movement (bull rush, reposition, …) doesn't affect the count.
