# Combat Quick Reference (PF1e)
Source: Core Rulebook, Combat chapter. See https://www.d20pfsrd.com/gamemastering/combat/. When in doubt, check the source and note the ruling in the live log.

## Starting combat
- **Initiative:** d20 + Dex + modifiers. Roll all enemies in one call.
- **Surprise round:** only combatants who were aware act, in initiative order, and each gets a standard or move action.
- **Flat-footed:** everyone is flat-footed until their first regular turn. They lose their Dex bonus to AC and can't make attacks of opportunity without Combat Reflexes.

## Actions per turn
One standard action and one move action, **or** one full-round action. Plus one swift action and any number of free actions. One immediate action per round, which uses up the next turn's swift action.
- **5-foot step:** only if you don't otherwise move that round. It doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity.

## Attacks
- A natural 1 always misses and a natural 20 always hits (attack rolls only; not skill checks).
- **Critical:** a roll in the threat range threatens a crit. Roll again with the same bonus; if that hits the AC, it's a crit.
- **Attacks of opportunity** are provoked by:
  - moving out of a threatened square (not a 5-foot step, and not the first square of a withdraw)
  - casting a spell
  - making a ranged attack while threatened
  - drinking a potion, standing up from prone, or retrieving an item
  - a combat maneuver made without its Improved feat
- **Casting defensively:** concentration check at DC 15 + 2 × spell level. If it fails, the spell is lost.
- **Damaged while casting:** DC 10 + damage taken + spell level.
- **CMB** = BAB + Str + special size modifier.
- **CMD** = 10 + BAB + Str + Dex + special size modifier.

## Modifiers
| Situation | Effect |
|---|---|
| Flanking | +2 on melee attacks |
| Charge | +2 attack, −2 AC until your next turn; move up to 2× speed in a straight line |
| Fighting defensively | −4 attack, +2 dodge AC (+3 with 3 ranks of Acrobatics) |
| Total defense | +4 dodge AC; no attacks |
| Cover | +4 AC, +2 Reflex |
| Concealment / total concealment | 20% / 50% miss chance (roll d%) |
| Prone | −4 on melee attacks; +4 AC vs ranged, −4 AC vs melee |
| Higher ground | +1 on melee attacks |

## Hit points
- **0 HP: disabled.** One move or standard action per round. A strenuous action deals 1 damage afterward.
- **−1 or below: dying.** Each turn, make a DC 10 Constitution check with a penalty equal to the negative HP total. Success means stable; failure means losing 1 HP.
- **Stabilizing someone else:** Heal DC 15, or any magical healing.
- **Dead** at negative HP equal to the Constitution score.
- **Nonlethal damage:** staggered when it equals current HP, unconscious when it exceeds current HP.
