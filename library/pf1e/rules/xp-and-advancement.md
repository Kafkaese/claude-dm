# XP & Advancement (PF1e)
Source: Core Rulebook, Gamemastering and Classes chapters. See https://www.d20pfsrd.com/gamemastering/ and https://www.d20pfsrd.com/classes/character-advancement/ (verified 2026-09-24).

## Awarding XP
- Total the **Total XP** of every defeated or overcome encounter (by CR), plus any story awards. Divide the total evenly among the PCs who took part.
- By RAW, NPC allies who fought (companions, cohorts) also take a share. For small or solo parties, follow `players/session-zero.md` on whether companions count.
- "Defeated" covers any way of overcoming the challenge: killing, routing, negotiating or sneaking past.
- The per-player columns are shortcuts that assume 3, 4–5 or 6 players. For odd party sizes, just divide the Total XP.
