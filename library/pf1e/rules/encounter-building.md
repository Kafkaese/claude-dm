# Encounter Building (PF1e)
Source: Core Rulebook, Gamemastering. See https://www.d20pfsrd.com/gamemastering/ (verified 2026-09-25). XP values are in `xp-and-advancement.md`.

## 1. Average Party Level (APL)
Average the PC levels and round to the nearest whole number. Then adjust for party size:
- **3 or fewer PCs:** subtract 1
- **6 or more PCs:** add 1

Companion NPCs who fight with the party count as party members for this.

## 2. Difficulty
| Difficulty | Encounter CR |
|---|---|
| Easy | APL − 1 |
| Average | APL |
| Challenging | APL + 1 |
| Hard | APL + 2 |
| Epic | APL + 3 |

## 3. Budget
The encounter's XP budget is the **Total XP** for its CR (from the XP table). Add creatures, traps and hazards until their combined XP reaches the budget without going over.

## 4. Guidelines
- **Mix difficulties.** Most encounters are easy to challenging. Save hard and epic ones for climaxes, and signpost them as session zero's encounter balance setting says.
- **Solo PCs are fragile, and the action economy decides fights.** Many weak enemies can be deadlier than one strong one, and vice versa for a single strong PC. Test the numbers against the PC's AC and HP.
- **Consider the "abilities to plan around"** in `dm/characters/`. Challenge them sometimes, not always.
- **Terrain and objectives** make an encounter interesting more than bigger numbers do. Give fights cover, hazards, elevation, and a goal other than "kill everything".
