# Archaeologist (Bard Archetype)
- **Source:** Ultimate Combat pg. 32 · https://aonprd.com/ArchetypeDisplay.aspx?FixedName=Bard%20Archaeologist · https://www.d20pfsrd.com/classes/core-classes/bard/archetypes/paizo-bard-archetypes/archaeologist/
- **Publisher:** Paizo
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-25
- **Tags:** archetype · bard

- **Bardic Performance:** not gained, nor any performance types.
- **Archaeologist's Luck (Ex):** swift action to activate. +1 luck bonus on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks and weapon damage rolls. **Rounds/day = 4 + Cha modifier** (no scaling with level). The bonus rises to +2 at 5th, +3 at 11th and +4 at 17th. Maintaining it is a free action; it ends immediately if the archaeologist is killed, paralyzed, stunned, knocked unconscious or otherwise prevented from taking a free action each round.
- **Clever Explorer (Ex), 2nd:** bonus of half his class level on Disable Device and Perception. Disables intricate/complex devices in half the normal time (min 1 round); opens a lock as a standard action. At 6th: can take 10 on Disable Device even if distracted or endangered, and **can disarm magical traps**.
- **Uncanny Dodge (Ex), 2nd:** as the rogue feature.
- **Trap Sense (Ex), 3rd:** +1, plus another +1 every three levels after 3rd (max +6 at 18th).
- **Rogue Talents, 4th:** one talent, plus one more every 4 levels after 4th.
- **Evasion (Ex), 6th:** as the rogue feature.
- **Advanced Talents, 12th:** at 12th and every 4 levels after, may pick an advanced talent instead of a rogue talent.

## Notes
- The rounds/day formula does NOT include "+2 per level after 1st", unlike bardic performance. Both aonprd and d20pfsrd agree.
- Magic-trap disarming comes only at 6th level. Before that, the archaeologist can't disarm magical traps (unlike a rogue's trapfinding).
