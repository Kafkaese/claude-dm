# Grasp
- **Source:** Heroes of the Darklands pg. 9 · https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Grasp
- **Publisher:** Paizo (Player Companion)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-25
- **Tags:** spell · divination · bard 0 (also arcanist, cleric, druid, hunter, magus, oracle, psychic, skald, sorcerer/wizard, warpriest, witch 0)

- **Casting Time:** 1 immediate action
- **Components:** V
- **Range:** personal · **Target:** you
- **Duration:** instantaneous
- **Saving Throw:** none · **SR:** no

Cast when you fail a Climb check that would make you fall. You immediately attempt another Climb check as a free action at a –2 penalty. Each further casting in the same situation adds another –2. Success stops the fall but gains no climbing progress.
