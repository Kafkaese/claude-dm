## Varisia at a glance
- Frontier region in northwest Avistan, about 346,684 square miles, from the Steaming Sea in the west to the Mindspin Mountains in the east. The south and southwest are lowlands, forests, grasslands and swamps; the northeast is the Storval Plateau and the Cinderlands.
- **No central government.** Independent city-states each hold a handful of towns; Shoanti tribes hold much of the wild interior.
  - **Korvosa:** largest city, founded 4407 AR as a Chelaxian colony, hereditary monarchy.
  - **Magnimar:** grew after 4608 AR when dissatisfied Korvosans moved there. About 50 miles south of Sandpoint.
  - **Riddleport:** pirate haven under an Overlord. **Kaer Maga:** near-anarchic city. **Janderhoff:** dwarven city in the Mindspin Mountains.
- **Peoples:** Varisians (wandering, descended from Thassilon's common folk), Shoanti (descended from its militia castes, pushed onto the Storval Plateau by Chelish colonization from 4405 AR onward), Chelaxians, plus Taldans, Ulfens and Tians.
- **Dangers:** giants, ogres, dragons, goblins and other monsters live close to the settlements. It is a frontier.

## Thassilon (DM background)
- Empire founded by Emperor Xin, an exile from Azlant. Ruled by seven runelords, each tied to a sin and a school of magic:

| Realm | Runelord | Sin | School |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shalast | Karzoug | Greed | Transmutation |
| Haruka | Krune | Sloth | Conjuration |
| Edasseril | Belimarius | Envy | Abjuration |
| Eurythnia | Sorshen | Lust | Enchantment |
| Cyrusian | Xanderghul | Pride | Illusion |
| Bakrakhan | Alaznist | Wrath | Evocation |
| Gastash | Zutha | Gluttony | Necromancy |

- **Earthfall** ended Thassilon in -5293 AR, roughly 10,000 years before the present.
- Thassilonian magic "wove" their structures, so many survive; giants were enslaved to build the cyclopean monuments. The language is **Thassilonian**.
- **Bakrakhan** (Alaznist, Wrath, evocation) had its eastern border on the Rasp, today Varisia's western coast. It warred constantly with Karzoug's Shalast; the Hellstorm Flumes along the coast are ruins of that conflict. Most of Bakrakhan, including its capital Xin-Bakrakhan (Hollow Mountain), sank beneath the Varisian Gulf at Earthfall. The Irespan in Magnimar survives from it.
- The Lost Coast (originally "the Rasp") was reshaped when the impact sank Xin-Bakrakhan and created the Varisian Gulf. Treating the Sandpoint coast as Bakrakhan borderland is a reasonable reading of the sources, not an explicit statement **[Claude inference]**.
- Rise of the Runelords spoilers (keep out of play unless that AP is run): the Old Light's true purpose and what lies beneath Sandpoint. Don't reveal or pre-empt these.

## The Lost Coast
- Coastal strip of southwestern Varisia from Magnimar north toward the Fogscar Mountains: "wild and desolate, yet beautiful", with lingering fogs, crashing waves, pine and nettle woods, and a rocky coastline of rugged bluffs.
- **Settlements:** Sandpoint, Magnimar; minor holdings Galduria, Nybor, Ravenmoor, Turtleback Ferry, Wartle, Wolf's Ear.
- **Notable sites:** Foxglove Manor, the Old Light, Thistletop, Windsong Abbey.
- **Inhabitants:** goblin tribes (the region's goblins are a byword for nuisance), ogre bands, ghouls. In the water: bunyips, reefclaws, great white sharks.
- **Lost Coast Road:** the coastal road between Magnimar and Sandpoint **[general knowledge; exact route details Claude invention]**.

## Sandpoint (4707 AR)
- Founded 4666 AR by four mercantile families, in a natural cove on the cliff-lined Lost Coast, on the Varisian Gulf. Population about 1,240 (89% human; halflings, elves, dwarves, gnomes).
- **Government:** Mayor Kendra Deverin; a seven-seat town council, four seats historically held by the founding families. Only the Deverins and Scarnettis still carry real weight. Titus Scarnetti heads the Scarnetti family (ambitious, owns mills).
- **Law:** Sheriff Belor Hemlock and the town guard at the garrison.
- **Religion:** Sandpoint Cathedral (new, replacing the chapel burned in 4702 AR), with a temple to Desna among its shrines; Father Abstalar Zantus.
- **Landmarks:** the Old Light, the Cathedral, the theater, the Kaijitsu glassworks, the garrison, the shipyards, the lumber mill, the Turandarok River.
- **The Late Unpleasantness (4702 AR):** murders by a killer called "Chopper", goblin raids, and the fire that destroyed the old chapel. Locals don't like to talk about it.
- **The Old Light:** built into a cliff in the northwest of town, about 120 ft of cliff plus a 50-ft ruined tower above it. Townsfolk think it was a lighthouse (markings near the top suggest a great light). Brodert Quink alone insists it was a weapon.
- **The Rusty Dragon,** 80 Market Street, just past the southern bridge over the Turandarok. Run by **Ameiko Kaijitsu** (former adventurer, famous cook, musician, eccentric hairstyles), with the halfling maid **Bethana Corwin**. More than a dozen tables, a stage, a hearth, a bar, and a rusted iron dragon on the roof. A dozen rooms named after dragon types (5 sp to 2 gp). Adventurers get discounts for telling a good story from the stage. Menu: spicy artichoke hearts stuffed with cheese curds, curried salmon, raspberry mead, barbecued abalone, seasonal ales.
- **Brodert Quink,** 130 Tower Street: human sage (expert 7), engineer and Varisian historian. Prickly, quick to anger, resentful of not being taken seriously, but generous to anyone who respects his theories or helps prove them. Frequents the Curious Goblin bookshop.

## The Dark Tapestry (for cosmic-weirdness flavor)
- The darkness between the stars "where inhabitants of Golarion believe true madness lies". Home to the Outer Gods and Great Old Ones, served by the Dominion of the Black. Contact brings "destruction and madness". Desna, whose shrine stands in Sandpoint's cathedral, is the goddess of stars and travelers.
- For a low-level one-shot, keep it at the level of hints: something far away, not something named.
