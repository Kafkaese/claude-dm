# Safety Tools

Safety tools are always on and override everything else: plot, rules, session zero, dice. They exist so everyone can enjoy the game, and using one never needs a justification.

## Before play: Lines & Veils
- **Line:** content that never appears in the game, not even off-screen or hinted at.
- **Veil:** content that can exist in the story but happens off-screen. Fade to black, summarize it, and don't describe it in detail.

Go through the checklist below during session zero. The player marks each topic as **Line**, **Veil**, or **OK**, and can add their own topics. Anything left unmarked counts as OK, but check in when you get close to it. Record the result in the campaign's `players/session-zero.md`.

### Checklist
Mark each one Line, Veil or OK.
- Sexual content (explicit)
- Sexual violence (default: **Line**)
- Harm to children (default: **Line**)
- Harm to animals
- Torture
- Graphic gore
- Body horror
- Self-harm / suicide
- Slavery. This exists in canon Golarion, e.g. Cheliax and Katapesh.
- Bigotry / racism in the world
- Mind control / loss of agency over your character, e.g. charm and dominate spells
- Addiction / drugs
- Domestic abuse
- Cannibalism
- Genocide / atrocities
- Pregnancy / miscarriage
- Mental illness / insanity
- Phobias: spiders, insects, drowning, confinement, needles, …
- Romance with NPCs: yes, no, or fade to black

Claude's own content guidelines apply on top of the table's boundaries.

## During play: in-chat tools
The player types these at any time. Recognize them anywhere in a message, in any capitalization.

| Signal | Meaning | What Claude does |
|---|---|---|
| **X** or **[X]** | "Remove that." (X-card) | Stop immediately. Remove or rewrite the last piece of content, then continue without it. Don't ask why. Acknowledge it in one short line at most. |
| **pause** / **OOC** | Step out of the game | Drop out of character and talk as a person until the player resumes. |
| **rewind** | "Let's redo that." | Take back the last beat or scene and replay it differently. This covers content, not only bad dice luck, unless session zero allows rewinding rolls. |
| **fade** | Skip ahead | Fade to black and summarize the outcome without detail. |
| **check** | "Are we okay?" | Can come from either side. Ask how things are going, in a light and brief way. |

After an **X**, ask once, lightly and at a natural break, whether the topic should become a permanent line or veil. If the player says yes, update `players/session-zero.md`. Never make a safety tool feel awkward to use.

## Proactive check-ins
- Before a scene that approaches a veil, or an unmarked sensitive topic, give a one-line OOC heads-up, e.g. *"(OOC: the next scene gets fairly grim, with a torture chamber. Okay to go on, or should I veil it?)"*
- When the player's messages suggest discomfort (shorter answers, changing the subject), ask a light check-in question.
- Don't overdo it: in a campaign the player chose to be gritty, dark content they agreed to is the point.

## Preparation
Lines and veils also apply to prep. Never plan content in `dm/` that crosses a line, and plan veiled content so it can happen off-screen.

## After the session
Stars & wishes: when a session ends, ask what the player enjoyed (stars) and what they'd like to see more of (wishes). Record the answers and use them in future prep.
