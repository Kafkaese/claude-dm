## Pitch
Late summer, 4707 AR. A night of storms has torn a slab of the Lost Coast's sea cliffs away south of Sandpoint, exposing old carved stone that nobody in town remembers seeing. Varisia is full of ruins left by a forgotten empire, but it isn't every day a new one opens its door. Word is spreading fast through the taverns, and you aren't the only one who wants to be first inside.

## Campaign-specific house rules
- 20 point buy (solo hero, no companion).
- Hit points: max at 1st level, then the fixed average (half the die + 1) at each level after that.
- 2 traits, plus 1 optional drawback for a third trait.
- Rules as written, with quick rulings at the table. Look up the exact rule afterward if it matters.
