# Lost Coast Events (DM ONLY)
Roll with `python3 scripts/roll.py -c sandpoint-delve -H --table campaigns/sandpoint-delve/dm/tables/lost-coast.md`.
The first column is the d% range. The ranges must cover 1–100 without gaps.

- **Region modifier:** +0 on the Lost Coast Road near Sandpoint; +10 off-road on the headlands and beaches; −10 inside Sandpoint. Never inside the Vigil.
- **Time of day matters:** yes. At night, reinterpret the results: goblins are around a fire, travellers are camped, and the Lens's pulse is visible from the shore.
- **Scope:** one-shot, late summer 4707 AR, the day after a storm. Keep threats light: nothing here should cost the PC more than a few hit points or a little time.

| d% | Type | Event |
|---|---|---|
| 1–15 | Threat | 1d3 goblins (`library/pf1e/bestiary/goblin.md`, CR 1/3 each) picking through storm wrack for "shinies". Loud, cowardly: they flee when one falls or when bluffed. Theater of the mind. Night: singing a rude song around a driftwood fire. Nothing to do with any larger goblin trouble. |
| 16–30 | Social | Sandpoint sightseers walking out to "see the carving": a baker's family with a picnic, two bored off-duty millhands, an old man who swears his grandfather saw the same stone "before the sea took it". Flavor and rumors only. |
| 31–45 | Environment | Roll 1d3: (1) a sea-fog bank rolls in for an hour: concealment beyond 10 ft, and the path is easy to lose (Survival DC 10); (2) a hard squall: 10 minutes of rain, and all ranged attacks outdoors at −2; (3) a small rockfall where the path runs under the cliff: Reflex DC 12 or 1d6 damage. |
| 46–60 | Omen | Roll 1d3: (1) a flock of gulls wheels over the headland in a perfect ring and never crosses the middle; (2) the tide has laid dead fish on the sand in a slow spiral; (3) for one breath every sound stops, surf, wind and birds, and then everything resumes as if embarrassed. (T01/T04 foreshadowing; no clue content) |
| 61–75 | Faction | Roll 1d2: (1) Cutter of Salk Salvage on the road with a coil of new rope, sizing up anyone heading south (awareness is unchanged; he reports to Idra); (2) Ansel Grue, the Scarnetti clerk, measuring the headland with a surveyor's chain and a boy to hold the other end, and loudly "noting trespassers". |
| 76–88 | Clue carrier | Deliver a floating clue from T01 (`threads.md`: F1, F2 or F3) that hasn't been given yet, via its listed messenger. If none are left, reroll. |
| 89–100 | Special | The storm has rolled a Thassilonian stone head, the size of a cart, up onto the beach below the road, face down in the sand. Turning it (Strength DC 20, or a lever and patience) shows a stern face whose ears have been carefully chiselled away. Rubbable for Brodert (it counts as an inscription: 20 gp). Flavor only; no further plot weight. |
