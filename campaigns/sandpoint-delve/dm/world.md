# World (DM ONLY)
The world's side of the story: what happens without the PCs, and how the world reacts to them. Consult it in every world turn. See `library/general/table-rules/living-world.md`.

- **World pressure:** lively (base event chance 20%, session budget ~2), scaled down for a one-shot. The scheduled events and triggers below do most of the work; random rolls are the seasoning.
- **Current region:** Lost Coast near Sandpoint (modifier +0; +10 off the road on the headlands; table `tables/lost-coast.md`). Sandpoint itself is a settlement (−10). **Inside the Vigil: no random events**, it's fully keyed.
- **Transition guide for this adventure:** Rusty Dragon → Gullstair = trip (10%). Sandpoint errands (e.g. to Brodert's house) = hops. The descent into the Vigil = no roll.

## Reaction triggers
Prepared "if → then" rules. Mark each one fired when it happens.
- [ ] If the PC asks around Sandpoint about the collapse → he hears of "the Magnimar lot" and that they drink at the Hagfish, and gets F1 if it hasn't been given (T01)
- [ ] If the PC deals with Idra → Awareness (Salk) 1: Cutter watches the ladder and counts what comes up (T01)
- [ ] If the PC intimidates or cheats Idra → Awareness (Salk) +1, and her attitude drops to unfriendly (T01)
- [ ] If the PC uses the shaft → Awareness (Brask) 3 at once: scenario C (T01)
- [ ] If the PC misaligns the Orrery Door or forces it → Awareness (Brask) 2: scenario B (T01)
- [ ] If the PC slides in the Tilted Hall, triggers a starfire tile or fights near the Vigil → no change; the surf covers it (T01)
- [ ] If the PC comes up the breach crack at low tide → Awareness (Brask) 1: scenario D (T01)
- [ ] If the PC enters the site after Hemlock closed it and is seen → Heat (Sandpoint) 1: a lecture and a 5 gp fine, forgiven if he brings up Brask or Jory's body (T01)
- [ ] If Brask and Pell both die with no witness → Heat (Sandpoint) 1: Hemlock wants a statement; Idra's or Hobb's word clears it (T01)
- [ ] If the PC removes or sells the Starwheel → Attention +1; Idra's buyer hears the PC's name (T03)
- [ ] If the PC covers the Lens and leaves the wheel in place → the Attention clock starts to drain; Idra calls it wise and lies to her buyer (T01, T04)
- [ ] If the PC tells the tale on the Rusty Dragon stage → the PC's name is known on the Lost Coast by morning (T02)
