# Timeline (DM ONLY)
The true chronology, including what the players don't know. Tag every entry `[locked SNN]` (the players know it) or `[flex]` (can still change). Check here before improvising anything about the past, or about who was where and when. See `library/general/table-rules/continuity.md`.

## Before the campaign
- ~ -5300s AR [flex] Bakrakhan's keepers build the Listening Vigil at Gullstair. In "the ninth year" they hear something through the Lens. The last keeper, Ithrazen, seals the shaft with a carved cap, locks the Lens with the Starwheel, paints the warning mural and leaves the niche.
- -5293 AR [canon] Earthfall. Thassilon falls; most of Bakrakhan sinks beneath the Varisian Gulf. The Vigil's entrance is buried in the reshaped cliff. [flex for the Vigil part]
- 4666 AR [canon] Sandpoint founded by four mercantile families.
- ~4687 AR [flex] Corin born to the elven scholar Sileth Vaerin; father unknown. [locked S00: Corin is an orphan]
- 4689 AR [flex] Sileth comes to Sandpoint with two-year-old Corin to survey the Lost Coast cliffs for Thassilonian sites. She hires Tamsin Reed's boat and pays her to mind the boy. She writes to Brodert Quink about "those who held the hours".
- 4690 AR [flex] Sileth walks the southern headlands and never returns; her fate is unknown. Tamsin keeps her satchel and raises Corin. [locked S00: Corin grew up in Sandpoint]
- 4702 AR [locked S00] The Late Unpleasantness (murders, fire) shakes Sandpoint. [flex] Corin, about 15, lives through it as a bystander, with no role in it.
- ~4702–4707 AR [flex] Corin grows up doing odd jobs (the Rusty Dragon stables, errands), borrows Brodert's books, and takes to delving the local sea caves and ruins.
- ~4695–4707 AR [flex] Gorrun Brask works for Idra Salk; his brother drowns on one of her salvage jobs.
- Early summer 4707 AR [flex] The coaster *Gray Merrow* wrecks on rocks north of Sandpoint.
- ~12 Arodus 4707 AR [flex] Idra Salk's crew arrives in Sandpoint to salvage the *Gray Merrow*; they lodge at the Hagfish.
- Night of 19/20 Arodus 4707 AR [locked S00] A violent storm collapses part of the cliffs south of Sandpoint, exposing carved stone. [flex: the place is Gullstair Point; the storm also cracked the shaft cap.]
- Dawn 20 Arodus [flex] Tamsin Reed sees the carved stone from her boat; the news reaches the docks by mid-morning. [locked S00: word is spreading]
- Afternoon 20 Arodus [flex] Salk Salvage rides to Gullstair, finds the broken shaft cap, camps and rigs the winch. Idra makes a rubbing of the cap.
- Dusk 20 Arodus [flex] Brask, Jory Fenn and Pell Cotter descend the shaft.
- Evening 20 Arodus [flex] Brask finds the Starwheel and starts listening to the Lens. The camp hears a hum.
- ~Midnight 20/21 Arodus [flex] Jory climbs back up to report; Brask cuts the rope; Jory dies on the Lens rim. Brask shouts his claim up the shaft. Tamsin sees the light pulsing inside the cliff from her boat.
- 21 Arodus, early morning [flex] Cutter and Hobb refuse to go down. Idra waits for a delver. A Scarnetti clerk hears of the find.
