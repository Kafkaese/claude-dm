## Player facts [locked S00]
- An orphan who grew up in Sandpoint.
- Wants to become a renowned explorer and adventurer: fame, fortune, and the secrets in Varisia's old stone.

## Hooks
- **Tamsin Reed** (the fisher from the hook scene) is the closest thing Corin has to family: she fed him, taught him boats, and cuffed him when he needed it. She's the one telling the story at the bar in scene 1, so she's a natural cameo and an easy source of floating clue F1. Give Corin the choice whether that history is "known" to him, and let the player shape it.
- **Brodert Quink** knows Corin as "the Reed boy who kept borrowing my books and returning them with sand in". That's a ready reason for Brodert to seek him out.
- **Ameiko Kaijitsu** knows him as a local lad (flavor only). A story told from her stage lands harder for a hometown boy (T02, fame).
- **The Late Unpleasantness** [canon, 4702]: Corin was about 15. He remembers the chapel fire and the fear. Don't tie him to canon victims or culprits, and don't touch Rise of the Runelords material.
- **After the one-shot** (dormant seed T05): his mother's fate and her satchel.

## DM-filled secrets [flex]
Check them against `timeline.md`; nothing here has been revealed.
- **What Corin knows:** Tamsin raised him from about age three, grudgingly and well, until he was old enough to fend for himself (odd jobs, the Rusty Dragon stables, Brodert's books). What Tamsin told him about his mother: *"An elf who owed me money."* His father is unknown to him.
- **The truth:**
  - His mother was **Sileth Vaerin**, an elven scholar and delver out of Magnimar. She came to Sandpoint in 4689 with her two-year-old son to survey the Lost Coast cliffs for Thassilonian sites. She hired Tamsin's boat, and paid Tamsin to mind the boy while she walked the cliffs.
  - In 4690 she went out on foot along the southern headlands and never came back. Her fate is unknown (keep it [flex]; don't decide it unless the game continues).
  - She was looking for "those who held the hours". Brodert's Magnimar fragment about keepers who "closed the glass" came to him through a letter from her, years ago. He never connected the half-elf boy with the elf who wrote it.
  - **Tamsin kept Sileth's satchel** in her loft for 17 years: a notebook in Draconic shorthand with Thassilonian sketches, including three stars in a line labelled Crown, Lantern and Ember, and a coastal map with a circle roughly where Gullstair is. She never mentioned it, half out of guilt (she spent the unpaid wages) and half out of fear of what it would send him after.
  - His father: left blank on purpose [flex].
- **How it can surface in the one-shot** (optional, at most one cameo, never required to win): Brodert, reading Corin's rubbings, remarks that the three-star mark "was in a letter I got, oh, twenty years ago. From an elf. Vaerin? Something like that." Or Tamsin, seeing the gold stars in Corin's rubbing, goes quiet and leaves early. Either one is a seed, not a scene.
