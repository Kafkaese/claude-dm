# Gorrun Brask
- **Role / occupation:** Idra Salk's long-time muscle and diver; now a deserter with ambitions
- **Threads:** T01
- **Stats:** Traitorous Brigand (NPC Codex p. 81), `library/pf1e/bestiary/traitorous-brigand.md`. 30 hp, AC 17. Carries the stat block's gear **except the potion of enlarge person**: he drank it last night to shift the Lens cradle (an adjustment, because the enlarged greataxe against a 25-hp PC was too swingy). With *bull's strength*: greataxe +8, 1d12+7.

## Appearance & voice
- Half-orc, big even before the potion, a sailor's tattoos faded to blue smudges, one tusk capped in cheap silver. Wet to the knees. His eyes have a faint blue-white after-image in them, like someone who stared at the sun.
- Slow, amused, oddly gentle. Since his night with the Lens he talks in a calm, almost dreamy way and hums its rhythm between sentences. The wit is dark and bone-dry.
- Lines: *"You came in by the door. Polite."* · *"It says a lot of things, if you let it. Mostly it listens. You'd be surprised how nice that is."* · *"Idra paid me in 'next time' for twelve years. This is next time."*

## Wants
- The Starwheel, and a new life a long way from Idra.
- To be listened to. The Lens has been the first thing in years that did.

## Hidden [flex]
- Cut the rope that Jory Fenn was climbing, around midnight on 20/21 Arodus. Jory died on the Lens rim.
- His grievance is real: twelve years of short pay, and his brother drowned on one of Idra's jobs.
- Plans to leave at the 5:30 p.m. low tide down the crack in the sea breach, then walk the beach south toward Magnimar. He'll take Pell, because Pell is too scared to refuse.
- Would kill the PC on the beach if the PC partnered with him.
- The Lens has not possessed him. He's just greedy, grieving and very tired, and it made all three feel reasonable.

## Whereabouts
- Dusk 20 Arodus: descended the shaft with Jory and Pell.
- Since then: in the Lens Chamber, prying at the Starwheel (Prize clock in `threads.md`).
- ~5:30 p.m. 21 Arodus: leaves via the breach if nothing stops him.

## Attitude toward the PCs
Curious rather than hostile at first. He'll parley before he fights unless the PC comes down the shaft or attacks.
