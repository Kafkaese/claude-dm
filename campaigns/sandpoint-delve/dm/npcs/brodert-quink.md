# Brodert Quink (canon)
- **Role / occupation:** Sandpoint's sage: engineer and Varisian historian, 130 Tower Street. Canon NPC (human expert 7); lore in `library/settings/golarion/regions/varisia.md`.
- **Threads:** T01, T02
- **Stats:** noncombatant (canon: human expert 7).

## Appearance & voice
- Elderly, wiry, ink on his cuffs, spectacles pushed up into white hair, a satchel of rubbing paper he carries like holy relics. [appearance details: Claude invention]
- Prickly and quick to anger [canon]. Talks in footnotes: *"Thassilonian, obviously; well, obviously to anyone who has read, which is nobody in this town."* Warms completely to anyone who respects his ideas [canon].
- Lines: *"They'll misspell your name, but still."* · *"The Old Light is a weapon. Nobody listens. Nobody ever listens."*

## Wants
- Rubbings, translations and proof, especially anything that supports his view that Thassilonian ruins were weapons or instruments rather than monuments.
- To be taken seriously, above all by Magnimar's scholars.

## Established [locked]
- [S00] (primer) The Old Light is a broken Thassilonian tower on the cliffs. Brodert himself isn't named in the primer.

## Hidden [flex]
- Knows a Magnimar fragment about "those who held the hours" who "closed the glass". He doesn't know what it means (floating clue F3 source).
- Can translate any Thassilonian rubbing in minutes. He pays 20 gp for rubbings, +30 gp for star-script.
- Received the "closed the glass" fragment and a three-star sketch by letter from an elven scholar, Sileth Vaerin, around 4689. He never connected her with Corin (seed T05).
- Knows Corin as a local lad who borrows his books.
- Seeing the Lens: he wants to, until he sees the PC's face. Then he doesn't.
- Canon caution: his theory about the Old Light is canon; its truth belongs to the Rise of the Runelords. Don't confirm or reveal it.

## Whereabouts
- Morning 21 Arodus: the Rusty Dragon (hook scene), then home.
- ~3 p.m. 21 Arodus: hires a cart and arrives at Gullstair Point, too impatient to wait.

## Attitude toward the PCs
Indifferent to prickly by default. Helpful if the PC shows respect for his work.
