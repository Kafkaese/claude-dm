# Idra Salk
- **Role / occupation:** salvage broker from Magnimar, owner of "Salk & Daughters Salvage" (there are no daughters; she painted them on the sign because it looks more trustworthy)
- **Threads:** T01, T03 (seed)
- **Stats:** noncombatant. If it ever matters: Bandit (`library/pf1e/bestiary/bandit.md`) with a club (whalebone cane) instead of the rapier and bow, Sense Motive +8, Bluff +10, Diplomacy +8. She won't fight.

## Appearance & voice
- Late fifties, Varisian-Chelaxian, grey hair cropped short, a salt-stained naval greatcoat far too good for her, reading glasses on a chain, a whalebone cane she uses as a pointer more than a support.
- Speaks like she's dictating a ledger entry: short, dry, every sentence a transaction. Never raises her voice. Finds other people's sentiment faintly expensive.
- Lines: *"Everything that goes into the sea comes out cheaper."* · *"I don't hire heroes. They want thanks. I pay; thanks is extra."* · *"Brask is a problem with an axe. I'm a problem with lawyers. Guess which of us wins in Magnimar."*

## Wants
- **The Starwheel,** for an unnamed Magnimar collector who pays 1,500 gp for Thassilonian gold. This matters more to her than anything else here.
- Brask punished, preferably by somebody else, preferably legally.
- Jory Fenn's body brought up. She means it: she'll pay his mother, and it's the one thing she won't haggle over.
- To be done before Sheriff Hemlock and the Scarnettis turn her hole into paperwork.

- Afternoon 20 Arodus: rode to Gullstair Point with her crew, set up camp and a winch at the shaft.
- 21 Arodus, all day: at the clifftop camp unless something forces her off it.

## Attitude toward the PCs
Indifferent, and interested because she needs a delver. She respects competence and a good haggle, and despises sentimentality she can't invoice.
