# Tamsin Reed
- **Role / occupation:** fisher, Sandpoint docks; owns the dory *Second Thoughts*
- **Threads:** T01 (floating clue F1), T05 (seed)
- **Stats:** noncombatant (if needed: Bandit, `library/pf1e/bestiary/bandit.md`, with a gutting knife instead of the rapier and bow)

## Appearance & voice
- Sixties, forearms like hawsers, a face like a walnut, a pipe she never lights. Drinks her ale warm.
- Talks in weather: short, grim, sideways affection. To Corin: *"You'll go anyway. You always go anyway. Take the good rope, not the rope you think is good."*

## Wants
- Corin alive and not doing the stupid thing. She knows he'll do it anyway.
- For nobody to ask about the satchel.
