# Campaign Plan (DM ONLY)
Solo one-shot, 1–2 sessions, level 3. Adventure: `adventures/the-listening-vigil/`. Everything below is `[flex]` unless tagged otherwise. Canon lore: `library/settings/golarion/regions/varisia.md`.

## Premise
[locked S00] A storm two nights ago tore away part of the sea cliffs south of Sandpoint and exposed carved Thassilonian stone. Word is spreading, and the PC isn't the only one who wants in.

[flex] The truth: the stone belongs to the **Listening Vigil**, a small sky-watch post of Bakrakhan (Alaznist's realm of wrath and evocation) cut into the headland at **Gullstair Point**, about two miles south of Sandpoint. Its keepers studied starlight as a weapon, the way the realm studied every fire. Their instrument, the **Lens**, a disc of black glass under a shaft open to the sky, showed them more than stars. Near the end, it showed them something far out in the dark between the stars that listened back. The last keeper, **Ithrazen**, capped the shaft with a carved stone slab, locked the Lens's cradle with the **Starwheel** (a gold-and-star-metal disc), painted a warning on the gallery wall, and left. Earthfall buried the rest.

The storm cracked the shaft cap on the headland top and sheared the cliff face open at the old entrance. For two nights the Lens has seen the sky again.

A Magnimar salvage crew got there first. Its broker, **Idra Salk**, sent her long-time muscle **Gorrun Brask** and two men down the shaft on the evening of 20 Arodus. Brask found the Starwheel, spent hours staring into the Lens, and decided twelve years of Idra's short pay ended tonight. When young **Jory Fenn** climbed back up to report, Brask cut the rope. Jory died on the Lens rim. Brask shouted up that the wheel was his and anyone following would meet his axe. Now he's prying the Starwheel loose, with the terrified deckhand **Pell Cotter** as his only company, and he plans to leave at low tide down a crack in the sea breach.

## Arc
One adventure, five beats (details in the adventure folder):
1. **Hook** (Rusty Dragon, morning 21 Arodus): rumors, Brodert Quink's commission, a fisher's tale of a light inside the cliff.
2. **Social encounter:** Idra Salk at the clifftop camp. Terms, information, a lie.
3. **Exploration encounter:** the Vigil: the cliff descent, the Tilted Hall, the Gallery of Hours (starfire tiles, the warning mural, the hidden niche), the Orrery Door puzzle.
4. **Combat encounter / climax:** the Lens Chamber. Brask, Pell, the pulsing Lens, the sea breach.
5. **Resolution:** the choice about the Lens and the Starwheel, Idra's deal, Hemlock at the top, Brodert, and the story told on the Rusty Dragon's stage.

If the game continues past the one-shot, see the dormant seeds in `threads.md`. Don't build further unless the player asks for more.

## Themes & Motifs
- **Old stone keeps old promises** [locked S00, folk wisdom]. The Vigil's promise is its warning, and it still holds.
- **Deep history leaking into the present:** a 10,000-year-old decision (Ithrazen's seal) undone by one storm and one greedy man.
- **Grey people, dry wit:** nobody is clean. Idra lies about what she wants, Brask has a real grievance, the sheriff serves a land-grabbing noble's paperwork.
- **The thing best left alone:** the Lens is never a fight and never explained. The heroic ending is being clever enough to walk away with the gold that isn't the key.
- **Recurring images:** the three gold stars (Crown, Lantern, Ember), a carved open hand (the keepers' sign: "we hold, we do not take"), rhythmic pulses of blue-white light, gulls that won't land near the shaft.

## Hooks & Seeds
The PC is **Corin**, a half-elf bard (archaeologist), an orphan who grew up in Sandpoint [locked S00]. Backstory centrality is a light touch (see `characters/corin.md`). He's a hometown boy, so the Rusty Dragon, Tamsin Reed and Brodert all know him. His mother is dormant seed T05, with at most one cameo line.
- **Fame:** Brodert Quink will name the PC in his letter to the Magnimar scholars. Telling the tale from the Rusty Dragon's stage earns a discount on the room (canon house custom; a free night for a great story is Ameiko's call) and a local reputation.
- **Fortune:** Idra's split, Brask's gear, the keeper's niche, the Starwheel (with strings attached).
- **Secrets:** the warning mural, the Lens vision, the lintel. An archaeologist who reads Thassilonian gets the most out of this ruin.
- **Rival:** Idra can become a recurring grey patron or rival if the game continues.
