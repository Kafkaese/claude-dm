# The Listening Vigil (DM ONLY)
Solo one-shot for a level 3 PC (half-elf bard, archaeologist). Effective APL 2 (solo: APL 3 − 1). One social, one exploration and one combat encounter, plus framing. Encounter details are in `encounters.md`, the battle map in `../../combat/maps/lens-chamber.txt`. Background and truth: `../../campaign-plan.md`. All content is `[flex]` except items tagged `[locked S00]`.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | Rusty Dragon, Sandpoint | framing | 8–9 a.m., 21 Arodus 4707 AR |
| 2. Idra's terms | Gullstair Point, clifftop camp | **social** | ~10 a.m. |

**Time pressure (soft):** Brask frees the Starwheel around **3 p.m.** and climbs out at **low tide, ~5:30 p.m.** Sheriff Hemlock reaches the headland around **2 p.m.** Brodert Quink shows up at the camp around **3 p.m.** A PC who goes straight there beats all of it with hours to spare. Detours cost time, but never close the adventure. See `../../world.md` for the schedule and triggers.

**Tides** [flex]: low ~5 a.m. and ~5:30 p.m.; high ~11:15 a.m. and ~11:40 p.m. Matters for falls into the breach (water or rocks) and for the beach route.

**Light:** Sandpoint and the clifftop are bright day. The Tilted Hall gets daylight through its crack. The Gallery of Hours and the Orrery Door are **dark** (the PC needs a light; the half-elf has low-light vision, not darkvision). The Lens Chamber is **dim light**: daylight falling 80 ft down the shaft, plus the Lens's own glow. Brask has darkvision.

## 1. Hook: the Rusty Dragon (framing)
**Strong start.** Don't open with "you're in a tavern". Open mid-argument.

> *Breakfast at the Rusty Dragon smells of curried salmon and wet wool; half of Sandpoint seems to have come in out of the drizzle to talk about the same thing. At the bar, a fisherwoman with forearms like hawsers is telling it for the fourth time: carved stone, a whole face of it, where the Gullstair cliff used to be. "And last night," she says, lowering her voice as if the sea might hear, "there was a light in it. Inside the rock. Blue-white. Coming and going, like breathing." Someone laughs. Nobody else does. Behind the bar, Ameiko Kaijitsu is polishing the same mug and watching you rather than the storyteller, the way a former adventurer watches a current one.*

What's here (pick what the player engages with, keep it brisk):
- **Tamsin Reed,** fisher (gruff, superstitious, drinks her ale warm). She more or less raised Corin (see `../../characters/corin.md`), so she's likely to tell *him* first, and to tell him not to go. Seed T05 lives in her loft; don't bring it up unless Corin shows her the three-star mark afterwards. Knows: the collapse is at **Gullstair Point**, about two miles south, off the Lost Coast Road down a sheep track. She saw the pulsing light from her boat around midnight (**floating clue F1**, see `../../threads.md`). "A Magnimar lot rode out yesterday with a cart and a winch. Salvagers. They'd strip a drowned man's teeth."
- **Ameiko Kaijitsu** (canon; warm, sharp, sardonic). Mentions the house custom [canon]: adventurers who tell a good story from the stage get a discount on their room. For a really good one she'll waive the bill [flex]. "Come back, mind. Dead men tell terrible stories." She knows Idra Salk's crew drinks at the Hagfish, not here, "which tells you enough".
- **Brodert Quink** bursts in, hat askew, carrying rubbing paper (see `../../npcs/brodert-quink.md`). He knows Corin ("the Reed boy who returns my books with sand in them") and makes straight for him. He's looking for anyone brave or stupid enough to go down there, since he is neither and his knees are worse. His offer:
  - **20 gp** for clean rubbings of any inscriptions, **+30 gp** for anything with star-script on it. He'll lend charcoal and paper.
  - **Fame:** he'll name the PC in his letter to the scholars of Magnimar "as the delver who recovered them. They'll misspell it, but still."
  - What he knows (free if the PC is respectful; if dismissive, he huffs and gives only the job): the old south cliffs were Bakrakhan borderland, the realm of wrath and fire, whose mages "studied every fire there is, including the ones in the sky". A Magnimar fragment speaks of "those who held the hours" and says they **"closed the glass"**. He doesn't know what that means.
  - If the PC brings him a rubbing later, he translates it (see encounters).
- **Leaving town:** the walk to Gullstair is a **trip** (about an hour). Run a world turn (`../../world.md`, table `../../tables/lost-coast.md`).

**If the PC ignores the hook:** the world acts. Tamsin's crew spreads the "light in the rock" story; by noon a Scarnetti clerk is talking about a claim; by afternoon Hemlock rides out. Deliver F1 or F2 through an outside actor (`living-world.md` stall ladder). It's a one-shot, so don't wait long.

## 2. Idra's terms: the clifftop camp (SOCIAL encounter)
Full encounter in `encounters.md` §1. Location: `../../locations/gullstair-point.md`.

> *The headland is all wind and sheep-bitten grass, ending in a fresh raw edge where the cliff simply isn't any more. A canvas lean-to snaps in the gusts. Beside it, a wooden winch stands over a black hole in the turf, ringed by a broken slab carved with a great open hand. The gulls wheel everywhere except over that hole. A grey-haired woman in a salt-stained greatcoat sits on a crate with a whalebone cane across her knees, reading a ledger as if she were at a desk in Magnimar. She doesn't look up. "If you're from the Scarnettis, the answer is no. If you're from the sheriff, the answer is also no, but politer. If you're a delver," and now she looks, "sit down. I've a job with a vacancy."*

Outcomes: a deal (split, gear, information), no deal (the PC goes it alone), or hostility. Every outcome leads into the Vigil. Routes in:
- **Rope ladder down the cliff face** to the Vigil's doorway (Idra's ladder, or the PC's own rope): the intended route, leading to the exploration encounter.
- **The shaft:** 80 ft straight down into the Lens Chamber, right in front of Brask. Signpost it as reckless: "Jory went down that way. So did his body, eventually."
- **The beach:** only near low tide. Climb the breach crack (Climb DC 15, 40 ft) directly into the Lens Chamber. Skips the exploration encounter.

## 3. Into the Vigil (EXPLORATION encounter)
Full encounter in `encounters.md` §2. Location: `../../locations/the-listening-vigil.md`.

Four stages: **the cliff descent**, **the Tilted Hall** (navigation), **the Gallery of Hours** (starfire tiles, the warning mural, the hidden niche), **the Orrery Door** (puzzle). The design goal: every obstacle has several solutions, and reading Thassilonian or studying the murals makes everything easier.

## 4. The Lens Chamber (COMBAT encounter, climax)
Full encounter in `encounters.md` §3. Map: `../../combat/maps/lens-chamber.txt`.

> *The door grinds aside on a breath of cold air that smells of salt and struck flint. Beyond is a round hall, far larger than the gallery. Grey daylight falls from a shaft high in the ceiling onto a dais, and on the dais lies a disc of black glass as wide as a cart, in a cradle of green bronze. Pinpoints of light move inside the glass. They aren't reflections: there's nothing above to reflect. The west wall is simply gone, and the sea breathes in and out through the gap. A man lies still beside the dais at an angle nobody sleeps in. Kneeling on the dais steps, working a pry bar under a gold wheel set into the bronze, is a very large half-orc, and he is humming along with the light.*

Brask would rather talk first unless he's been alerted or attacked. The encounter is a fight with exits: Pell can be turned, Brask bargains when he's losing, the Lens can be covered or turned into a weapon, and the breach is a hazard for everyone.

## 5. Up into the light (resolution)
Resolve by what the PC did. Don't moralize; let the NPCs react in character.

**The Lens and the Starwheel** (track in the Attention clock, `../../world.md`):
- **Covered and wheel left in place:** the pulses stop. The chamber is just a cold room with a black mirror in it. Attention drains to 0 by the next dawn, faster if the PC asks Hemlock or Idra to have the shaft capped again. This is the "leave it alone" ending. It's quiet, and it should feel like the right kind of quiet.
- **Wheel taken:** the Lens "opens" (Attention +1). The wheel is worth a fortune (see Rewards). If the Lens stays uncovered at nightfall, Attention +1 again.
- **Lens broken** (it takes effort: hardness 5, 30 hp, or a DC 20 Strength check with a lever): Attention +1. The sound goes out, not just around. After that there's nothing left to listen through.
- **If Attention reaches 4/4:** the epilogue. For three nights, anyone in Sandpoint who looks at the sky over the gulf counts one more star than their neighbor does, and argues about it. Then it's gone. Brodert doesn't sleep for a week. Nothing attacks anyone. Log it as the dormant seed T04. Never pre-empt the Rise of the Runelords: this is a different, smaller thing.

**Idra Salk** (see her file for her attitude):
- With the wheel: she pays the agreed split within the hour, from a purse that's clearly been ready since yesterday.
- Without the wheel, but with Brask dealt with and/or Jory's body brought up: she's disappointed, dry, and fair. "You left gold in a hole because a window frightened you. …Probably wise. I'll lie to my buyer; I'm better at it than you." She pays **25 gp** for Jory's body, "for his mother, not for you".
- Brask alive and captured: she wants him handed to Hemlock, not to her. "I'd only be tempted."
- Doublecrossed by the PC: Awareness (Salk) 2. She has no muscle left to act on it this week, but she remembers (seed T03).

**Sheriff Belor Hemlock** (canon; arrives ~2 p.m., so he's likely at the top when the PC climbs out): a decent, tired lawman. He wants facts, the body, and Brask if alive. A Scarnetti clerk (flavor NPC, Ansel Grue, all ink and entitlement) with him asserts Titus Scarnetti's claim to "anything under Scarnetti grass". Hemlock's ruling if the PC argues (Diplomacy DC 15 or a decent argument): "The grass is Scarnetti's. The hole under it isn't written down anywhere. Take your finds, file a report, and don't make me regret it." If the PC entered after Hemlock closed the site, Heat 1: a lecture and a 5 gp fine, forgiven if the PC brings Brask up.

**Brodert Quink:** (T05 cameo option: on seeing the three stars, he mutters that an elf named Vaerin sent him that mark in a letter twenty years ago.) Pays for rubbings (20 gp, +30 gp for star-script: the lintel, mural and door all qualify), translates anything, and is transported by the mural. If told about the Lens, he wants to see it, then, after one look at the PC's face, decides he doesn't. He writes the letter naming the PC.

**The Rusty Dragon stage:** the PC can tell the tale for a room discount [canon custom], or a free night and meal if it's a great one [flex]. A good telling (Perform or Diplomacy DC 15, or just a good in-character telling) means the story is all over Sandpoint by morning: the PC's name is known on the Lost Coast. That is the fame.

**If Brask escapes with the wheel:** Idra pays nothing and leaves for Magnimar at dawn. A week later a traveller mentions a half-orc selling gold in Magnimar's Underbridge and then not being seen again. Log it as seed T03/T04.

## What if the PC fails or retreats
- **Retreats before the combat:** the clocks keep running. Brask frees the wheel (~3 p.m.) and leaves at low tide (~5:30 p.m.). The PC can intercept on the beach below Gullstair (use the same stat blocks; theater of the mind or `--blank 20x12` with rocks as `^` and surf as `~`), or tell Hemlock, whose deputies catch Brask on the Lost Coast Road the next day (off-screen; no reward from Idra, but Hemlock's goodwill).
- **Retreats mid-combat:** Brask doesn't chase past the Orrery Door (he doesn't want to leave the wheel). He finishes prying and leaves at low tide. Pell may follow the PC out if he's been talked to.
- **Dropped to 0 or below:** mercy applies (session zero, low levels). Roll stabilization honestly first. Brask doesn't deliver a coup de grace; he takes the PC's coin purse, says "Tell Idra the wheel sends its regards," and goes. Pell, unless he's dead or fled, pours Brask's potion of cure moderate wounds into the PC (or stabilizes him) and gets him up the rope ladder. The PC loses the prize but keeps his life and can still go back down and cover the Lens. This is a real loss, not a punishment: Idra pays nothing.
- **Falls into the breach:** at high tide (around noon), the surf is deep: the first 20 ft are free, then 1d3 nonlethal per 10 ft (so 2d3 nonlethal); Swim DC 15 to reach the crack, then Climb DC 15 back up. Near low tide (5 a.m. or 5:30 p.m. ± 1 hour) it's rocks: 4d6. Warn about this before the PC takes a reckless risk at low tide.
