# The Listening Vigil: Encounters (DM ONLY)
Effective APL 2 (solo level 3). Budgets from `library/pf1e/rules/encounter-building.md`. All `[flex]`.

---

## §1 Social: Idra's terms (Gullstair Point camp)
**People:** Idra Salk (`../../npcs/idra-salk.md`); crewmen **Cutter** (young, sullen, rope-burned palms: he was holding the rope when it went slack) and **Hobb** (old, half deaf, a Desna medallion he keeps touching). Both use the Bandit stat block (`library/pf1e/bestiary/bandit.md`) without bows or horses, but nobody here wants a fight.

**Idra's stance:** indifferent, but she needs the PC. She opens with an offer, not questions. She never begs.

### What she tells for free (it's in her interest)
- Brask, Jory and Pell went down the shaft at dusk yesterday on the winch rope. Around midnight the rope went slack, with Jory on it. They heard him hit.
- Then Brask shouted up the shaft: *"There's a star-wheel of gold down here, Idra, and it's mine. Twelve years of short pay. Anyone else comes down that rope gets the axe."* Then the hum started again.
- There's a doorway on the cliff face, about 40 ft down from the edge, "carved like a coffin stood on end". She has a rope ladder ready for it and nobody willing to use it.
- **Her rubbing** of the shaft cap: an open hand over three stars in a vertical line, with Thassilonian words beside each star and a line beneath (they read *Crown*, *Lantern*, *Ember*, and *"The Watch opens. The glass is closed. Hold; do not take."* with Linguistics DC 15, automatically if the PC reads Thassilonian, or via Brodert). This solves the Orrery Door.

### Her offer ladder
The base offer comes unprompted. Negotiating is a short exchange of arguments, and then **one Diplomacy check** (don't make the player roll repeatedly). Good arguments (the sheriff is coming, "you've nobody else", the risk) give +2 each at the DM's judgement, up to +4.
| Result | Terms |
|---|---|
| Base (no check, or a failure) | Rope ladder and lantern. The PC keeps "anything else" he finds; **30%** of the wheel's sale (her buyer pays 1,500 gp, so 450 gp). Conditions: the wheel to her, Brask "dealt with, I don't care how", Jory brought up if possible. |
| Diplomacy DC 17 | **50%** of the wheel. She adds Brask's habits: *"He carries a tall draught, a potion that makes him bigger, so watch his reach. And he swings like he's chopping wood; don't stand where he can reach you twice."* And about Pell: *"Nineteen, never won an argument with a door. Talk to him and he'll fold."* (Her information is stale: Brask already drank the enlarging potion last night. Let the PC over-prepare.) |
| Diplomacy DC 22 | **60%**, plus 2 *potions of cure light wounds* from her crate, and she admits the buyer exists (an unnamed Magnimar collector; she won't name him). |
| Fails by 5 or more | She closes at the base offer: *"I've haggled with drowned men who had more to offer."* |

- **Intimidate** (DC 17): she agrees to the DC 17 terms, then Awareness (Salk) +1, her attitude drops to unfriendly, and she will try to shave the payment later.
- **Bluff** vs her Sense Motive +8. She's hard to fool about money and easy to fool about her own safety.
- **PC's Sense Motive** (roll hidden with `-H`):
  - DC 15: she wants the wheel far more than her men back. *She hasn't once asked if Pell is alive.*
  - DC 20: she already has a buyer, and it isn't her knee keeping her up here; the hum frightens her.
- **Hobb, privately** (Diplomacy DC 10, or just kindness): *"Brask weren't right when he got down there. Humming. Same tune as the hole."* If the PC skipped Idra entirely, Hobb can instead carry floating clue F2 to the PC.
- **No deal:** she doesn't stop him. *"The ladder's mine. The cliff's the Scarnettis', apparently. The hole belongs to whoever's dumbest."* The ladder costs 10 gp, or Cutter pulls it up and the PC uses his own rope (Climb DC 5 with a rope braced against the cliff) or free-climbs (DC 20).
- **If the PC wants to use the shaft:** she'll winch him down for free ("Your funeral, my rope"). Signpost it: straight into the Lens Chamber, in front of Brask, who is waiting for exactly that. Awareness (Brask) goes to 3 as soon as the rope moves.

**XP:** 600 (story award, CR 2) for any meaningful resolution: a deal, a clever refusal, or information obtained.

## §2 Exploration: into the Vigil
Location details: `../../locations/the-listening-vigil.md`. The Vigil is fully keyed: **no random events inside**. Time in the ruin: about an hour if the PC is careful.

**Corin and Thassilonian:** he reads Draconic, the root of the script, but not Thassilonian itself. Wherever this file says "Linguistics DC 15, automatic if the PC reads Thassilonian", Corin rolls Linguistics (+6) with a **+4 circumstance bonus**, taking 1 minute per inscription, no retry. On a failure by 4 or less he gets the gist but not the exact wording; on a failure by 5 or more he gets a wrong reading (roll hidden). His Knowledge (history) is +6.

### A. The descent and the Threshold
- **Rope ladder:** no roll. **Own rope:** Climb DC 5. **Free climb** on fresh rockfall: DC 20. Fail by 5 or more: fall from halfway (20 ft, 2d6) onto the doorway ledge, then Reflex DC 15 or go over the edge (a further 40 ft into the surf: see the falls rule in `README.md`). Warn the player before a free climb: this is a reckless choice.
- The ledge is 10 ft deep, strewn with fresh rubble. The doorway is tall and narrow, wider at the foot, "like a coffin stood on end".
- **Lintel inscription** (Thassilonian; Linguistics DC 15, automatic if the PC reads Thassilonian; rubbing for Brodert): *"Walk as the stars walk. Hold the hours. The glass is closed."*

> *Out of the wind at last. The doorway breathes cold, still air that smells of wet stone and something like a snuffed candle. Beyond it the dark goes back further than the headland should allow.*

### B. The Tilted Hall (navigation)
40-ft passage. The storm cracked it lengthwise: the floor now tilts about 15° toward a fissure along the seaward wall, 2 ft wide, with daylight and spray coming up through it.
- Crossing: **Acrobatics DC 12** (wet, tilted), or **Climb DC 10** along the landward wall's carved ledge, or no roll with a rope and a bit of time (pitons or a line secured at the doorway).
- Fail by 5 or more: slide. **Reflex DC 12** to catch the fissure's lip; otherwise drop 10 ft into the fissure (1d6) and get wedged: Climb DC 15 or Escape Artist DC 12 to get out. The surf covers the noise, so Brask hears nothing.
- Flavor: bronze wall brackets shaped like open hands, palms up, holding nothing.

### C. The Gallery of Hours (traps, mural, hidden niche)
60 ft long, 15 ft wide, vaulted 20 ft. **Dark.** The floor is a mosaic: black tiles (the void) with inlaid white-and-gold star tiles forming constellations that touch one another like stepping stones. The walls are painted in four panels.

**Starfire tiles: 2 × Burning Hands Trap (CR 2 each)**, reskinned (`library/pf1e/bestiary/burning-hands-trap.md`). At 20 ft and at 45 ft into the gallery, carved open hands jut from both walls at chest height, palms out. Reskinned trigger: **location**, meaning any weight of about 10 lb or more on the **black tiles** within 10 ft of a pair of hands (instead of the official proximity trigger; same CR). It triggers a blue-white flash from the palms: 2d4 fire, Reflex DC 11 half (trap sense applies).
- **Clues:** Perception DC 15 spots faint, ancient scorch marks on the black tiles near the hands, and none on the star tiles. Knowledge (arcana) DC 15: the palms are carved evocation foci. The lintel said *"walk as the stars walk"*.
- **Bypass:** walk only on the star tiles. No roll if the PC goes slowly and deliberately; **Acrobatics DC 10** if hurrying, carrying something awkward, or in the dark with a poor light.
- **Trigger it safely:** throw or drag something heavy (a rubble chunk, a pack on a rope) onto a black tile in the zone from outside the 15-ft cone. The palms flash harmlessly, and the trap doesn't reset (reset: none), so that zone is safe afterwards. Anyone who has noticed the scorch marks or read the lintel can work this out; if the player asks whether the tiles look weight-triggered, Perception DC 15 or Disable Device DC 15 confirms that the black tiles sit a hair lower than the star tiles.
- **Find the triggers:** Perception DC 26 pinpoints the exact tiles.
- **Disable:** not possible for Corin. It's a magic trap, and Clever Explorer only covers magical traps from 6th level (`library/pf1e/character-options/archaeologist-bard.md`). Disable Device can still confirm the weight trigger (DC 15, above). *Dispel magic* (CL check DC 11) would suppress it, if he somehow has access.
- **Just run it:** 2d4 fire per zone, Reflex DC 11 half (+1 trap sense, +1 luck if active). Survivable, and it costs little but hit points.
- **XP:** 600 each, whether bypassed, disabled or survived.

**The mural** (four panels; captions in Thassilonian, Linguistics DC 15 or automatic if the PC reads Thassilonian; without reading, Knowledge (history) DC 15 gets the gist). Rubbings count as star-script for Brodert.
1. Keepers in blue robes around a black disc under an open shaft, the sky full of stars. Three stars are picked out in gold leaf, one above another, and one keeper's hand rests on a ringed door. *"We hold the hours of the sky."* (Knowledge (history) DC 15, or reading: the gold stars are the door's key.)
2. The painted sky has a hole in it: not painted black, but carved out of the plaster, deeper than the plaster should go. The keepers kneel with their ears to the glass. *"In the ninth year, a star that was not a star."*
3. The keepers turn away with their hands over their ears. One walks calmly into the sea. *"It does not speak. It listens. It has learned to wait."*
4. A single keeper sets a gold wheel into the disc's bronze rim, and a stone slab carved with an open hand is lowered over the shaft. *"I, Ithrazen, last of the hours, close the glass. The key stays in the lock. What I kept, I leave in my own hand."* Beneath the panel, a carved open hand stands slightly proud of the wall.
- **Knowledge (arcana) DC 20** after seeing the mural: the disc isn't a telescope but a listening post, a scrying focus turned outward into the dark between the stars. Scholars agree that some things in the Dark Tapestry notice being watched.

**The keeper's niche:** press the carved hand under panel 4 (the caption says to), or Perception DC 20 to spot the seam (the archaeologist's clever explorer bonus applies). A stone drawer slides out, containing:
- a deep-blue mantle embroidered with silver constellations, supple as if woven last year: a *cloak of resistance +1* (1,000 gp; faint abjuration)
- 3 Thassilonian gold "hour-coins", square with a hole in the middle (25 gp each to collectors)
- a bronze tube holding a gold-foil star chart with one star scratched out (150 gp; Brodert would trade a great deal of goodwill for it)

### D. The Orrery Door (puzzle)
A round bronze door 10 ft across, set with three concentric rings that turn stiffly. Each ring bears seven Thassilonian star-glyphs. Above them, a carved open hand, palm down, points at the top position. The door opens when **Crown** (outer ring), **Lantern** (middle) and **Ember** (inner) all sit under the hand. The last 10 ft of mosaic before the door show three gold stars in a vertical line under an inlaid hand.

**Ways to solve it** (any one is enough):
- Idra's rubbing, or the mural's panel 1 plus reading or Knowledge (history) DC 15.
- The mosaic before the door: Perception DC 12 to notice that the three gold stars are raised, then Linguistics DC 15 or Knowledge (arcana) DC 15 to match them to the glyphs (automatic if the PC reads Thassilonian).
- **Disable Device DC 25:** feel the tumblers seat, 1 minute (30 seconds with clever explorer).
- **Brute force:** Strength DC 25. It's loud (treat as a wrong alignment).
- Trial and error: each wrong try counts as a wrong alignment.

**Wrong alignment:** the rings ring like a struck bell and a breath of killing cold spills from the seams: 1d6 cold to anyone adjacent (Fort DC 12 half). **Brask's awareness goes to 2** (scenario B below). Further wrong tries only do the cold.

**Listening at the door** (Perception DC 10): a rhythmic hum that rises and falls; metal scraping on metal; a young man's voice, *"Can we go now? Please?"*, and a deep, amused one, *"Not yet. It's nearly out. Listen to it, Pell. It's pleased."*

**Opening it:** the rings click home and the whole door rolls aside into the wall, grinding. That isn't silent, but at awareness 0–1 Brask is absorbed in the wheel and doesn't react in time. See §3.

**XP:** 400 (story award) for opening the door by any means.

## §3 Combat: the Lens Chamber (climax)
**Map:** `../../combat/maps/lens-chamber.txt` (22×16). Read the `//` legend lines at its top first.
**Budget:** Brask (Traitorous Brigand, CR 2, 600 XP) + Pell (Bandit, CR 1/2, 200 XP) + the Listening Lens (hazard, CR 1, 400 XP) = **1,200 XP, CR 4**: hard for effective APL 2. That's appropriate for the climax, because it has several release valves: Pell can be turned (the fight is then CR 3), the Lens can be covered (then 800 XP), and Brask bargains at 10 hp. Stat blocks: `library/pf1e/bestiary/traitorous-brigand.md`, `bandit.md`, `listening-lens-hazard.md`.

### Terrain
- **The Lens** (L8–M9, impassable): bronze cradle, waist high. The **dais steps** (K7–N10) are difficult terrain.
- **Pulse** at initiative 0 every round: affects everyone within 10 ft of the Lens. Squares: K6 L6 M6 N6, J7–O7, J8 K8 N8 O8, J9 K9 N9 O9, J10–O10, K11 L11 M11 N11. Will DC 12 or **staggered 1 round**; failing by 5 or more also means **dazzled 1 minute**.
  - If the Lens is **opened** (Starwheel removed): 15 ft and DC 14. The extra ring of squares is K5 L5 M5 N5, J6, O6, I7–I10, P7–P10, J11, O11, K12 L12 M12 N12.
  - **Turn the Lens:** standard action adjacent + DC 15 Strength, DC 15 Disable Device or DC 17 Knowledge (arcana). The next pulse is a 30-ft line in a chosen direction instead: save as above, plus 1d6 cold on a failure.
  - **Cover the Lens:** full-round action adjacent, with a cloak, sack or tarp (Brask's sack is at D12). The pulses stop.
  - Brask knows the rhythm and tries to end his turn outside the ring. **Perception or Sense Motive DC 12** (once, hidden): the PC notices him stepping back just before each pulse.
- **Keeper's Gallery** (row 2): 10 ft up behind a waist-high balustrade (row 3). Attacks from the floor get −4 against someone behind it (cover). Reached via the **stairs** at U3–U4 (east, next to the door) or the **rubble slope** at B3–C4 (west). Jumping down is Acrobatics DC 15 to take no damage, otherwise 1d6.
- **The sea breach** (A7–D12): 40-ft drop. Tide matters (see `README.md`, falls). A bull rush (Brask's CMD 17) or a failed pulse save while fighting at the edge won't push anyone by itself, but a creature that is staggered at the edge and bull-rushed goes over. **The crack** at B13–D13 is Brask's way down (Climb DC 15, 40 ft).
- **Tide pools** (E12–G13): water, and slick: Acrobatics DC 10 to run or charge through.
- **Storm rubble** (H13, I13, I14, R11, S11, S12): difficult. **Pillars** (F5, Q5, Q13): cover.
- **Light:** dim everywhere (shaft daylight plus Lens glow). Pell has normal vision, so the PC may get concealment on the gallery if the PC's light is out; Brask has darkvision.
- **Jory's body** lies at J7 (in the pulse ring). Brask's sack and a coiled rope sit at D12, ready for the climb.

Pell may start `--hidden` if the PC's light doesn't reach the gallery (reveal him when spotted: Perception DC 12, or when he shoots). Track the Lens pulse manually at the end of each round (initiative 0) and note it in the tracker line.

### Brask before blows (scenario A and usually B)
He's been listening to the Lens for 14 hours. He's calm, amused, and slightly wrong.
- *"You came in by the door. Polite. Jory came by the rope. Rude of him to leave the same way."*
- *"It said someone would come down the stairs. It says a lot of things, if you let it. Mostly it listens. You'd be surprised how nice that is."*
- **His offer:** a third of the wheel if the PC helps pry it out and carry it down the crack at low tide. He means to kill the PC on the beach. PC's Sense Motive vs Brask's Bluff +1 (roll hidden).
- **His grievance** (true): twelve years of Idra's short pay, and his brother drowned on one of her wrecks "for a crate of Chelish candlesticks. She sent flowers. Cheap ones."
- **Talking him out of the wheel** entirely: Diplomacy DC 25 (he's hostile). +5 if the PC explains the mural (part of him already knows). On a success, he leaves at low tide without the wheel, keeps his gear, and tells Pell to go with the PC. A peaceful ending is legitimate: don't force the fight.
- **If the PC accepts his deal:** Brask is civil until the breach. When the PC starts down the crack, Brask attacks (the PC is climbing and loses his Dex bonus; uncanny dodge prevents that). Same map, fought at the breach.

### Tactics
- **Brask:** (his *enlarge person* potion is already used.) He opens with Intimidate to demoralize (+9), then wades in with the greataxe, using Cleave where he can. He keeps the PC close and ends his turns outside the pulse ring when possible. **Power Attack only once he's below half hp.** Mercy note: don't combine Power Attack with a PC at 5 hp or less unless the fight has been going very badly for Brask. He'll drink *cure moderate wounds* once below 15 hp if he has a quiet moment, and *bull's strength* only if he started the fight at range and has a spare round (already drunk in scenario B).
  - **Morale:** at 10 hp or less he bargains: *"Half. Fine. A third. Gods, just let me walk."* If refused, he tries to escape down the crack, with the wheel if it's free. Orc ferocity lets him fight one round below 0.
  - **If the wheel is at 5/6** on the Prize clock (see `../../threads.md`), a full-round action wrenches it free. He'll do this if the fight turns against him: the Lens opens (DC 14, 15 ft) and Attention +1.
- **Pell:** shoots from the gallery (cover +4 behind the balustrade), using Point Blank Shot within 30 ft. He doesn't want to be here.
  - **Turning him:** a standard action of honest appeal, Diplomacy **DC 12**, Intimidate **DC 12**, or a Bluff that makes sense (DC 12). On a success he lowers his bow and backs away. If the PC mentions Jory (Pell saw the cut), he gets +4. If the PC's result is 20 or higher, Pell shoots Brask once, then hides.
  - He surrenders automatically the first time he takes damage, or when Brask falls. If Brask is winning and threatens him, he keeps shooting.
- **The Lens:** pulses at initiative 0, indifferent to sides. The PC can turn it into a weapon or cover it (see Terrain).

### After the fight
- `combat.py end`, then a checkpoint. Loot: see `README.md` Rewards.
- **Jory's body** can be carried out (Idra pays 25 gp "for his mother").
- **The wheel:** in the cradle at M10's rim. Freeing it takes 1–3 full-round actions depending on the Prize clock (Strength DC 15, or Disable Device DC 15 which does no damage to the mechanism). See the Lens choices in `README.md` §5.
- **Looking into the Lens deliberately** (not the pulses): Will DC 14.
  - **Success:** a vision of the keepers' last night, and a certainty that something very far away has paused to consider the PC's existence. +2 insight on Knowledge checks about Thassilon for the rest of the adventure.
  - **Failure:** confused for 1 round, then shaken for the rest of the day, and the PC hears his own name in the surf that night.
  - Either way, Attention +1.
