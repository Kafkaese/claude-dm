# The Listening Vigil [Claude invention]
- **Region / parent location:** inside the headland of Gullstair Point, Lost Coast
- **Threads:** T01

## Description
A small Thassilonian sky-watch post of Bakrakhan, cut into the headland roughly 10,000 years ago. Four areas, entered from the cliff-face doorway (encounter details in `adventures/the-listening-vigil/encounters.md`):
1. **The Threshold:** a rubble ledge 40 ft down the cliff face, before a tall, narrow doorway. Lintel inscription.
2. **The Tilted Hall:** a 40-ft passage cracked lengthwise by the storm, tilting toward a spray-filled fissure. Bronze wall brackets shaped like open hands, palms up.
3. **The Gallery of Hours:** 60 × 15 ft, vaulted, dark. A star-mosaic floor (black void, white-and-gold stars), two pairs of carved palms jutting from the walls, a four-panel mural, and a carved hand under the last panel.
4. **The Orrery Door:** a round bronze door with three turning rings of star-glyphs under a carved hand, palm down.
5. **The Lens Chamber:** a round hall under the shaft, with the Lens on its dais, the Keeper's Gallery 10 ft up behind a balustrade, and the west wall broken open to the sea. Map: `combat/maps/lens-chamber.txt`.

Sensory: cold still air; wet stone and a smell like a snuffed candle; the surf as a constant low roar; in the Lens Chamber, a hum that rises and falls with the blue-white pulses.

## Hidden [flex]
- **Purpose:** keepers of Bakrakhan watched the sky here, as part of the realm's study of every kind of fire. The Lens let them listen into the dark between the stars, and something there listened back. The last keeper, Ithrazen, sealed the shaft with the carved cap and locked the Lens's cradle with the Starwheel.
- **Traps:** starfire tiles, 2 × Burning Hands Trap reskins (Gallery of Hours).
- **Door solution:** Crown (outer), Lantern (middle), Ember (inner) under the hand.
- **Keeper's niche:** under mural panel 4. It holds a *cloak of resistance +1*, 3 hour-coins and a gold-foil star chart.
- **The Starwheel:** gold and star-metal disc, 1 ft across, set in the Lens cradle. It is the "key" that keeps the Lens closed. Worth 800 gp as metal and art, 1,500 gp to Idra's buyer.
- **The Lens:** hazard (`library/pf1e/bestiary/listening-lens-hazard.md`). Nothing comes through it; the danger is in being noticed (Attention clock, `world.md`).
- Anything not listed here isn't here: no other rooms, no undead, no second treasure.
