# Gullstair Point [Claude invention]
- **Region / parent location:** Lost Coast, about two miles south of Sandpoint. An hour's walk down the Lost Coast Road, then a sheep track west to the headland.
- **Threads:** T01

## Description
- A grassy headland, sheep-bitten turf and gorse, ending in a fresh, raw edge where a slab of cliff fell into the sea two nights ago. The rubble fan below is still pale, unweathered stone.
- **The shaft:** a black hole about 6 ft across in the turf near the edge, ringed by the three broken pieces of a carved stone cap (an open hand over three stars in a line). A wooden winch stands over it. Cold air rises from it, and on the pulse a faint hum. **The gulls wheel everywhere except over it.**
- **Idra's camp:** a canvas lean-to, crates, a cart, two hobbled horses that won't graze near the hole, a cookfire that keeps blowing out.
- **The cliff face:** 40 ft below the edge, the Vigil's doorway (tall and narrow, "like a coffin stood on end") opens onto a rubble ledge. Another 40 ft below that is the surf.
- **Exits:** the sheep track east to the Lost Coast Road (Sandpoint 2 miles north); down the ladder to the doorway; down the shaft (80 ft) into the Lens Chamber; the beach below, at low tide only, reached by a goat path a quarter mile south.

## Established [locked]
- [S00] (pitch/primer) A storm two nights before the adventure collapsed part of the cliffs south of Sandpoint and exposed carved stone. Word is spreading, and others want to be first inside.

## Hidden [flex]
- The shaft drops straight into the Lens Chamber, landing beside the Lens.
- At night, the Lens's pulse is visible from the sea as a faint blue-white light inside the cliff, through the breach.
