# Character Creation
- **Level / starting wealth:** 3rd level, 3,000 gp of starting gear (the standard wealth for level 3)
- **Ability scores:** 20 point buy
- **Hit points:** max at 1st level, then the fixed average (half the die + 1) per level
- **Traits / drawbacks:** 2 traits, plus 1 optional drawback for a third trait
- **Allowed sources:** Core Rulebook plus the main Paizo rulebooks (APG, UM, UC, ACG, OA, Unchained, …)
- **Banned / restricted options:** none. You're solo, so options that help you survive alone are welcome.
- **Useful roles / campaign-relevant skills:** you'll be on your own, so self-sufficiency matters. Expect use for:
  - Knowledge (history, arcana, dungeoneering), Linguistics
  - Perception, Disable Device
  - Diplomacy / Bluff / Sense Motive
  - some way to handle a fight with several enemies
- **Backstory hooks:**
  - Why are you in Sandpoint right now?
  - What drives you: fame, fortune or the secret itself?
  - Is there a scholar, patron or rival whose opinion matters to you?
  - Have you heard of Thassilon, the empire whose ruins dot Varisia?

The player's character: a level 3 half-elf Bard (Archaeologist), a young man chasing fame, fortune and secrets as a dungeon-delving adventurer. Register him with `/add-character`.
