# Setting Primer
What the characters know about the world and the starting region: common knowledge only, no secrets.

## The world
- **Golarion, 4707 AR.** Absalom Reckoning counts the years since the city of Absalom was founded.
- **Varisia** is a wild frontier in northwest Avistan. It has no king, just a handful of city-states (Magnimar, Korvosa, Riddleport) and wide stretches of hills, forest and moor between them.
- **Varisians** are a wandering people known for caravans, music, dance and fortune-telling (harrow cards). **Chelaxian** colonists came from the south and built most of the towns. **Shoanti** tribes hold the northern highlands.

## Thassilon
- Some ten thousand years ago an empire of sorcerer-lords ruled here. The Earthfall, a catastrophe from the sky, ended it.
- Its ruins are everywhere: giant statues, spiral stone towers (the **Old Light** in Sandpoint is one), and monuments carved with an angular script few people can read.
- Scholars in Magnimar and Korvosa pay well for rubbings and relics. Treasure hunters pay better, if they live.
- Folk wisdom: *"Old stone keeps old promises."* Most people leave ruins alone.

## Sandpoint
- A small, prosperous coastal town of about 1,200 people on the **Lost Coast**, a few days north of Magnimar.
- **Landmarks:** the Old Light (a broken Thassilonian tower on the cliffs), the brand-new Sandpoint Cathedral, the **Rusty Dragon** inn (run by Ameiko Kaijitsu), the Kaijitsu glassworks and the lumber mill.
- **Authority:** Mayor Kendra Deverin and Sheriff Belor Hemlock. Both are decent, practical people.
- **History:** five years ago a string of murders and a fire, the "Late Unpleasantness", shook the town. People still don't like to talk about it.
- **Nearby:** the Lost Coast cliffs and their sea caves, the Sandpoint Hinterlands, and goblin tribes in the brambles who are more nuisance than threat.

## Right now
- It's late summer and storm season is starting. A violent storm two nights ago collapsed part of the cliffs south of town, exposing carved stone.
- Everyone in Sandpoint has an opinion about it, and a few people are already making plans.
