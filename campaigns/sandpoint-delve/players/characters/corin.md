## Permanent & situational modifiers
- **Elven Immunities:** immune to magic sleep effects; +2 on saves vs. enchantment spells and effects
- **Archaeologist's Luck:** swift action, 7 rounds/day (4 + Cha). +1 luck bonus on attack rolls, saves, skill checks and weapon damage rolls while active. *(Not included in the numbers above.)*
- **Clever Explorer:** +1 (half level) on Disable Device and Perception *(already in the totals above)*. Disables complex devices in half the time; opens a lock as a standard action. Can't disarm magical traps until 6th level.
- **Uncanny Dodge:** can't be caught flat-footed and keeps his Dex bonus to AC even against invisible attackers
- **Trap Sense +1:** +1 on Reflex saves vs. traps, +1 dodge bonus to AC vs. traps

## Notable abilities
- **Cantrips of note:**
  - *detect magic*
  - *light*
  - *grasp* (Heroes of the Darklands; immediate action: when a failed Climb check would make you fall, reroll at –2)
- Other bard spells come up in play when they're used.
- No bardic performance (the Archaeologist gives it up for Archaeologist's Luck).

## Backstory (player version)
An orphan who grew up in Sandpoint. Corin wants to make a name for himself as a renowned explorer and adventurer: fame, fortune and the secrets buried in Varisia's old stone.
