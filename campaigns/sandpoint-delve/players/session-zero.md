# Session Zero
What the table agreed on. The DM follows this, and anyone can ask to revisit it.

## Frame
- **Scope:** very short one-shot to test the system. Exactly one social, one exploration and one combat encounter, plus the framing story. The combat is complex enough to use the battle map (`scripts/combat.py`).
- **Setting:** Golarion, Varisia, Sandpoint and the Lost Coast; late summer 4707 AR
- **Content:** original, written by Claude
- **Players:** solo  **Companions run by DM:** none

## Inspirations
- **Loved works:** The Stormlight Archive, Malazan Book of the Fallen, Lovecraft, the First Law trilogy, Neil Gaiman's books
- **Disliked works / tropes:** none named
- **Must-haves:** none named. Dungeon delving fits the character.
- **Takeaways:** deep ancient history leaking into the present; morally grey, somewhat cynical NPCs with dry, dark humor; old cosmic things that are best left alone; myth hiding just under the everyday world. The lead tone stays heroic: the PC can win and matter.

## Tone & Style
- **Theme:** heroic, with undertones of ancient mystery and a touch of cosmic weirdness
- **Flavor:** dungeon delving (Thassilonian-era ruins)
- **Structure:** mostly linear (a test one-shot)
- **World pressure:** lively, scaled down to one-shot size
- **Branching:** focused (0–1 active side plots)
- **Pillar balance:** balanced, with one encounter of each pillar
- **Intrigue & mystery:** some

## Challenge & Consequences
- **Lethality:** standard. Death is possible but rare.
- **DM mercy / fudging:** mercy at low levels only (about levels 1–3), so it applies to this one-shot
- **Reckless choices:** realistic but warned
- **Encounter balance:** mostly balanced; avoidable fights are clearly signposted

## Rules
- **Starting level:** 3  **Advancement:** none planned (medium XP track if the game continues)
- **Ability scores:** 20 point buy
- **Allowed sources:** Core Rulebook plus the main Paizo rulebooks (APG, UM, UC, ACG, OA, Unchained, …)
- **Variant / house rules:** max HP at 1st level, then fixed average; 2 traits plus 1 optional drawback
- **Dice:** the player rolls their own dice; Claude rolls for NPCs and monsters. Secret checks (Perception, Sense Motive, …) are rolled hidden by Claude.
- **Rules approach:** RAW, with quick rulings when needed

- **NPC dialogue:** voice NPCs directly, with distinct voices and dry wit (in the spirit of First Law).
